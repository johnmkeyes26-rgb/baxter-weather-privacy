Baxter Weather: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Baxter Weather (“the app”) is published by John Keyes. If you have any questions about this policy, contact johnmkeyes26@gmail.com.

What the app does

The app shows the weather for places you choose, along with a cartoon dog, Baxter, who reacts to the conditions. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Information stored on your device

  • The places you save (a name you choose, the city, and its coordinates)
  • Your temperature unit (°C or °F)

This information stays on your device. We do not receive it or store it on our own servers.

Information sent to other services

  • Weather and city search (Open-Meteo.com): when you search for a city or load a forecast, the app sends your search text or the coordinates of a saved place to Open-Meteo. As with any internet request, this includes your IP address. See open-meteo.com for their policy.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): the free version of the app shows ads. Google and its partners may collect your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, and device and usage information to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent first (for example in the UK and EU), and on iPhone it asks for your permission before tracking you across other companies’ apps and websites.
  • Purchases: if you buy the option to remove ads, the payment is handled by Apple or Google, and we do not see your payment details. A purchase-management service may receive an anonymous identifier and your purchase status so that the purchase can be restored.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not collect your name, your email address, or your precise location.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Delete saved places in the app, or remove the app to clear its stored data.
  • Reset your device’s advertising identifier, or limit ad personalisation, in your device settings.
  • Decline tracking or consent requests when the app asks.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top will change when we do.
